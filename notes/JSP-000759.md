## Lean statement and interpretation

Lean 4 / Mathlib v4.32.0. Standalone source:
`~/math4/jsp/JSP/JSP000759.lean`, copied byte-for-byte to
`units/jsp/lean/JSP000759.lean`; imported by `~/math4/jsp/JSP.lean`.

```lean
open scoped Classical in
theorem leonard_counterexample :
    ∃ H : SimpleGraph (Fin 57),
      H.edgeFinset.card = 141 ∧
      ∀ u v, u ≠ v → ¬ ∃ ps, InternallyDisjointPaths H u v ps
```

The namespace is `JSP000759`. The concrete theorems are `G_edge_count` and
`G_no_five`. `InternallyDisjointPaths G u v ps` requires an injective family
`ps : Fin 5 → G.Walk u v`, each walk to satisfy `IsPath`, and any common support
vertex of distinct paths to equal one of the endpoints. This is vertex-internal
disjointness, matching Leonard's “no points in common, save the endpoints”
on page 283. Injectivity explicitly expresses five distinct paths: without it,
five copies of the direct edge would vacuously have disjoint empty interiors.

The graph is defined by the explicit 141-edge list. A kernel-checked equality
identifies `G.edgeFinset` with the corresponding unordered-edge finset, and
kernel reduction proves its cardinality.

Each cut includes a bitmask for a vertex set R containing u and excluding v.
The kernel checks every graph edge: an edge crossing R either touches S or is
the direct endpoint edge. This finite closed-set check certifies non-reachability
after removing S and the direct edge. R was computed by Python BFS, but its
correctness is independently checked in Lean and is not trusted. The lemma
`walk_meets_separator` proves by induction that closed-set non-reachability
forces every walk to meet the separator. `nonreachable_induce_meets` also
explicitly derives this conclusion from non-reachability in the induced graph
on vertices outside S. The adjacent case additionally proves
that an S-avoiding simple path is exactly the direct edge.

`no_five_of_cut` assigns each path either a separator vertex in its interior or
the unique direct-edge slot. Disjoint interiors and distinct paths make that
assignment injective; at most four slots cannot accommodate `Fin 5`. All 1596
cuts are verified with `decide +kernel`, in batches of at most 29 pairs.
Reversing paths covers the opposite endpoint order. There are no `sorry`s,
custom axioms or native evaluation tactics.
